WIRS Accredited Potable & Fire Main Design

Good water network design keeps a development on programme and on budget, and gets you to adoption without surprises. Aquamain are accredited with WIRS design and offer a free design service on all our build contracts.

If you are an SLP, ICP, developer, consultant or contractor that needs a WIRS design for your project, we offer a standalone service. We can supply you with a design for you to submit for approval with any Incumbent or NAV.
