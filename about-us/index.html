Understanding the basics

Self-Lay (SLP) & Independent Connection Provider (ICP)

What is a Self-Lay Provider (SLP)?

If your development or building project requires a new water network, you have the option to have this carried out by the water company, or to work with an approved third-party contractor like us to do the contestable work — commonly known as self-lay, or SLP. (WIRS accredited.)

What is an Independent Connection Provider (ICP)?

An ICP is an accredited company that can build electricity networks to the agreed standards and quality required for them to be owned by either a Distribution Network Operator (DNO) or an Independent Distribution Network Operator (IDNO). (NERS accredited.)
