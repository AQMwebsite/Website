Utility Infrastructure for I&C Projects

Industrial and commercial sites bring bigger loads, tighter programmes and more complex network design than standard housing. Aquamain delivers the water and electricity infrastructure these schemes depend on — from initial advisory and design through construction, commissioning and adoption — all under one independent, accredited contractor.

Water for commercial sites

Under our WIRS accreditation we design, construct and commission water networks for commercial and industrial developments, including larger-diameter mains, fire and sprinkler mains, metered supplies and diversions.

High and low voltage electricity

Our NERS accreditation covers LV and HV jointing and cable laying up to 33kV, plus substation installation — the capability that commercial and industrial loads typically demand. We work with DNOs and IDNOs to deliver the right connection and the most cost-effective adoption route for your site.
