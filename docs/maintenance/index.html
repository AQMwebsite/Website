Ongoing Maintenance for Water & Electricity Networks

Once a network is built and adopted, it needs to be kept safe, compliant and in service. Aquamain provides ongoing maintenance programmes for both water and electricity networks — operating under framework agreements to carry out planned and emergency works, routine maintenance and alterations to existing networks for NAVs and IDNOs.

Two ways we keep networks running

Planned and emergency, both covered

Planned maintenance

Scheduled, preventative works — inspections, servicing and routine repairs planned around your programme to keep adopted networks compliant and reliable.

Emergency works

Rapid response to faults, leaks and outages — keeping water and power flowing when something goes wrong.
