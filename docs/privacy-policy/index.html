Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data we collect through our website, aquamain.com, why we use it, who we share it with, and your rights. Last updated: 9 November 2026.

Who we are

Aquamain (UK) Ltd is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. We are registered in England and Wales, company number 05248553, at Unit 22, Evercreech, Shepton Mallet, Somerset BA4 6NA. We are registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office, registration number ZA303318.

You can contact us about your personal data at info@aquamain.com or on 01749 345842.

What this policy covers

It covers visiting the site, using our contact and careers forms, emailing us through the addresses shown on the site, and the marketing emails we send to business contacts.

What we don’t do

Our website sets no cookies and shows no advertising. We don’t sell your data or pass it to other companies for their own marketing. We don’t make decisions about you by automated means.

Marketing emails

We sometimes send emails about our services and news to business contacts who have enquired with us or worked with us. We do this because it is in our legitimate interests to keep in touch with the businesses we work with.

You can stop these emails at any time, free of charge, by replying to ask us to stop or by emailing info@aquamain.com. We’ll then keep your email address on a do-not-contact list, only so that we don’t email you again.

We may also share personal data where the law requires it, or to protect our legal rights.

Transfers outside the UK

Some of our providers are based in the United States, so your data may be transferred there. GitHub, Cloudflare, Resend and Microsoft are each certified under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. UK law recognises this as giving your data an adequate level of protection.

Cookies

Our website does not use cookies. Our Cookie Policy explains this in more detail, including how our analytics works without them.

Keeping your data secure

The website is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and the forms send your details over that encrypted connection. Our forms only accept submissions made from our own website. Access to our email is limited to the staff who need it.

If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will report it to the Information Commissioner’s Office within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. If it is likely to put you at high risk, we will tell you directly without undue delay.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used, which is what this policy is for
  • get a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • have inaccurate data corrected
  • have your data deleted
  • restrict how we use your data
  • object to us using your data on the basis of legitimate interests
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it
  • receive your data in a portable format, where this applies

To use any of these rights, email info@aquamain.com. We’ll respond within one month. It’s free in most cases.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

Links to other websites

Our website links to our LinkedIn page. LinkedIn and any other site we link to have their own privacy policies, which apply once you go there.

Changes to this policy

We’ll update this policy when the way we handle personal data changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
